# Project guidelines

- Keep the complete quiz in `index.html`, including its CSS, JavaScript, and question data.
- Do not add servers, build tools, external dependencies, fonts, or network requests.
- Keep the interface and question content in Korean.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, screen-reader feedback, and accessible score information.
- Respect `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`, including JavaScript-driven animations.
- Keep changes scoped to the requested behavior.
- After quiz changes, check all 10 questions, correct and incorrect answers, score calculation, progress, results, and restart. For animation changes, also check reduced motion.
- Do not commit credentials, local configuration, or personal workshop notes.
